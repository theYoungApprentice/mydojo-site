Privacy Policy — My Dojo

Last updated: 2026-11-02 · Package: com.youngapprentice.mydojo

My Dojo ("the app") is a local-first Android application for managing a martial arts dojo (members, grades, lesson calendar, attendance, membership fees, exam eligibility, and technical glossary). This policy explains how the app handles user, member, and device data.

Summary

My Dojo operates on a local-first architecture with optional cloud synchronization for dojo instructors and collaborators. All business and personal data entered into the app is owned exclusively by the Dojo Administrator (Maestro). The developer does not collect, sell, or monetize user data. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.

Data Processed by the App

  • Dojo configuration: dojo name, martial arts discipline, role settings, and an optional dojo logo image picked from your device.
  • Member records (PII): names, contact details (email, phone), guardian details (for minors), belt/grade history, attendance records, membership fee status, and medical certificate expiration dates entered by the dojo administrator for administrative and sports safety compliance.
  • Instructor notes & deshi diary: technical evaluations and private notes.

Security & Encryption

  • Hardware-backed Keystore: Cryptographic keys protecting the local database and authentication tokens are anchored to the device's hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • Field-level Client Encryption: Sensitive personal identifiers (such as emails, phone numbers, guardian contact information, and personal notes) are encrypted client-side using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption before transmission or synchronization.
  • Transport Security: All communication with backend synchronization services (Supabase) occurs strictly over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Tenant & Role Isolation: Cloud storage enforces Row-Level Security (RLS) and cryptographically signed JWT claims to ensure that data belonging to one dojo can never be accessed, queried, or modified by another dojo or unauthorized role.

Children's Data & Health Information

Minors (Under 18): Martial arts dojos often instruct minors. Details concerning minors (names, birth dates, guardian contact details) are entered exclusively by the dojo administrator or instructors solely for course organization, emergency contact, and association membership requirements.

Medical Certificate Dates: In accordance with sports regulations, the app allows tracking the expiration dates of non-agonistic/agonistic medical fitness certificates. The app does not process medical diagnoses or health records; only the certificate validity period is tracked to safeguard athlete safety during training.

Data Retention and Deletion (GDPR)

Users and Dojo Administrators retain full control over their data at all times:

  • Local Storage: Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes all data from the device.
  • Cloud Records: Dojo Administrators can permanently delete their dojo directly from the application settings, triggering an immediate and atomic cascade deletion of all cloud records across all tables.
  • Subject Rights: Inquiries or data deletion requests can be addressed directly to the dojo administrator or to the developer contact below.

Third-Party Services

When cloud synchronization is configured, the app communicates with Supabase (PostgreSQL/PostgREST cloud infrastructure) strictly as a data processor for backend persistence and authentication (Email OTP/Magic Link). No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
